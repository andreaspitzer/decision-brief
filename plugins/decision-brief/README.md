# decision-brief

A skill that turns a pending decision into a decision brief you can act on. The brief leads with the ask and a recommendation, then gives the context, a concrete scenario, a visual, a graded evidence register, a decision matrix and an expert panel with a dissenter. Claude sizes the brief to how much the decision matters: Light, Standard or Deep.

Ask "brief me on …", "help me decide …", "should we …" or "decision brief". The skill works with no setup. An optional `decision-brief-profile.md` in your project adds your organisation's rules: who decides, locale, sources, tripwires and channels.
