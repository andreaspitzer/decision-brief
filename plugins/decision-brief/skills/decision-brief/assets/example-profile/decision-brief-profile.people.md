# Decision-brief profile, people branch: Quillmere Bakery Cooperative

Fictional example. Applies when the job, hours, pay, hiring, performance or role of a named person is part of the decision. The core's people floor always applies; this file adds what Quillmere sets.

## 1 Basis

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Legal basis | Employment contracts and the cooperative's privacy policy (section 4) |
| Jurisdiction | Ontario, Canada |

## 2 Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Storage and delivery | A private document in the "People" folder; in the chat only the link, the tier and the deadline |
| Visibility | First header line: "Visible to: the general manager and the board chair" |
| Delete after | 12 months after the decision, or as the privacy policy says if shorter; the header carries the date |
| Index row | Open row titled "People decision" plus deadline; decided row names role and shop, never the person |
| Background agents and research | Never used for people decisions |

## 3 Legal frame

General points to check, not legal advice. Put them in the legal section when relevant.

| Point | What it means for the brief |
|---|---|
| Ontario employment standards | Hours, overtime and notice periods follow the provincial rules; check before any change to hours or pay |
| Cooperative bylaws | A worker-owner's removal needs a board vote; say so in the header |
| Privacy policy | Use data for this decision only |
| Outside advice | For dismissal or discipline, the brief names the cooperative's lawyer as a step before deciding |
