# Decision-brief profile: Quillmere Bakery Cooperative

Fictional example. Quillmere is an invented worker-owned bakery cooperative with 4 shops and 1 central kitchen in Ontario, Canada. The core skill holds the format and method; this file holds everything that belongs to Quillmere.

## 1 Scope and role

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Organisation | Quillmere Bakery Cooperative: 4 shops, 1 central kitchen, 38 worker-owners |
| In scope | Any Quillmere decision |
| Out of scope | Personal decisions of worker-owners; decisions of the regional bakers' association |

## 4 Categories

Guidance only, not defaults.

| Category | Typical domain sections | Typical visual | Typical panel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Products and recipes | Allergen and label impact; cost per unit in CAD | Cost per unit before/after | Head baker; shop lead; a regular customer |
| Suppliers and purchasing | Minimum term and exit; delivery reliability | Cost over 3 years per option | Treasurer; head baker; kitchen lead |

| People and roles | See the people branch | Rota or org chart before/after | Worker-owner rep; treasurer; outside HR adviser |
| Money and budget | Variance against the yearly budget; cash runway in weeks | Bridge from budget to proposal | Treasurer; board chair; outside accountant |
| Food safety and risk | Event timeline; likelihood × impact | Timeline; risk grid | Food-safety lead; insurer contact; shop lead |

## 5 Tier tripwires

| Hard to reverse if any of | Wide reach if any of |
|---|---|
| A signed contract or lease longer than 12 months | Customers see it: prices, recipes, opening hours |
| Printed labels or packaging change | More than one shop changes how it works |
| A worker-owner's job, hours or pay changes | It needs a board vote (spend over CAD 10,000) |
| Equipment over CAD 25,000 that cannot be resold | It needs a vote at the members' general meeting |

## 6 Sources and their limits

| Source | Use | Limits and handling |
