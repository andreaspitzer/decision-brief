# Decision-brief profile: Fernhill Cycling Club

Fictional test profile. Fernhill is an invented amateur cycling club in Galway, Ireland, with 140 members and a fleet of 24 rental bikes.

## Who calls the shots

Siobhán Keane, club treasurer, decides everything under EUR 5,000. Above that, the committee of 7 decides and Siobhán Keane is the accountable decider.

## Money, time and writing

- Euros (EUR)
- Irish time, 24-hour clock
- Irish English spelling

## When it is a big deal

- Hard to reverse: anything we sign for more than 12 months; selling or scrapping club property.
- Wide reach: anything rental customers notice; anything that changes the members' fee.
